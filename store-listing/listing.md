# WhoseTurn: store listing

## App name (max 30)
WhoseTurn

## Short description (Play, max 80)

## Subtitle (App Store, max 30)
Plan the family week together

## Full description (max 4000)
Most family apps are trackers: a list of chores and a tick box. WhoseTurn is a planner. It helps your family sit down once a week, decide together who does what, and share the load fairly, before the week starts.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Set up your family's routines and to-dos once.
2. On your planning day, plan the week together in one simple table.
3. Each morning, everyone sees exactly what's theirs today.

PLAN THE WEEK
• One weekly table for everything: tap a box to choose who does it, tap again to switch
• See how the load is shared, person by person and day by day, and balance it before anyone feels overloaded
• Unplanned days stand out, so nothing falls through the cracks
• Plan this week, or get next week ready
• A weekly planning day, with a reminder, so planning becomes a family habit

PLAN MEALS & GROCERIES
• Plan lunch and dinner for the week, and who cooks
• Keep a backlog of family favourites, or spin for an idea
• A shared grocery list: what you need, what you have

ROUTINES & TO-DOS
• Daily, weekly or specific-day routines, grouped by category
• One-off to-dos with an owner and a target date
• Mark anything 🔒 personal so only you see it

TODAY
• One simple list of everything due today, for you or the whole family
• A progress card that shows how the day is going
• Drag to put things in the order you like

TRACK, THEN PLAN BETTER
• See how the plan went for everyone
• The last 8 weeks at a glance, by category and by person

MADE FOR FAMILIES
• Invite family members with a code
• Morning reminders to check today's list
• Sign in with Google or Apple; no passwords to remember
• No ads. Delete your account at any time from Settings.

## Reviewer notes
Sign in with any Google account (or Sign in with Apple on iPhone). No invite code, payment or second device is needed. After signing in, tap "Create a family". Add routines and to-dos from Today → Create, and assign them on the Assign tab. Account deletion: Settings → Delete my account.
